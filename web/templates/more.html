About Biscuit

Biscuit helps teachers take attendance in BigBlueButton classes: it compares the saved user list with your class list and tells you who was absent.

  • Names are matched ignoring case, spaces and punctuation, so “DEVI .P.T” matches “Devi P.T”.
  • Absentees with a near-identical attendee name are flagged with “maybe joined as…”.
  • Files are processed in memory and never saved on the server.
